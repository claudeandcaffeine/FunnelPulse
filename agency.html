⚠ Critical Finding

Your email upgrade campaign is cannibalizing the free trial path

The upgrade-nudge email sequence has a 10.7% CVR vs 15% on the free trial activation email. But it's driving 3.6x the revenue per click because upgrading users have higher AOV. However, the overall CAC on the upgrade sequence is ₹1.43 vs ₹0.33 on the free trial — suggesting you're overspending to convert people who would have upgraded organically.

Action: Cut upgrade-nudge spend by 30% and redirect to free trial activation, which has the better efficiency.
