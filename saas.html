⚠ Critical Finding

Your LinkedIn ads have great CTR but terrible trial conversion

CTR is 1.8% (healthy for LinkedIn), but only 0.3% of visitors start a trial. Every impression is wasted after the click. The landing page doesn't match the audience intent from LinkedIn — it's built for cold search traffic, not warm social professionals.

Action: Build a separate landing page for LinkedIn traffic with a case study first, then the trial CTA.
