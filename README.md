🚀 Why FunnelPulse?
Most dashboards just show you numbers. FunnelPulse interprets the relationships between them. It catches issues like:
- Your ad is compelling, but your landing page is killing the conversion.
- You're paying a premium for clicks, but your unit economics are broken.
- Your ROAS looks healthy, but your LTV:CAC ratio is in the danger zone.

✨ Features
- 5-Stage Analysis: Awareness, Interest, Conversion, Revenue, and Efficiency.
- 100+ Diagnostic Rules: Automatic cross-metric evaluation.
- Executive PDF Reports: One-click generation of professional diagnostic reports.
- Multi-Campaign Compare: Side-by-side analysis of up to 3 campaigns.
- Privacy First: 100% client-side. Your data never leaves your browser.

🛠 Tech Stack
- Pure HTML5 / CSS3 (Apple-inspired Dark Mode)
- Vanilla JavaScript (Zero dependencies)
- Standard CSV parsing for easy imports
